# Too many tabs

Two browser extensions for taming your tabs, and for handing them to your AI agent. Both are free and open source.

## Agent Browser Bridge

Let Claude Code, Cursor or any MCP agent use the browser you're already in, with your real tabs and logins. It can tidy your tabs, dig through history and bookmarks, and click around pages, and you can chat to it from Chrome's side panel. Works with Chrome and Safari.

- Download: https://github.com/lx223/agent-bridge-browser-extension/releases
- Source: https://github.com/lx223/agent-bridge-browser-extension

## JumpAround

Chrome's tab bar gives up at about twenty tabs. JumpAround gives you Cmd+K (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux) and fuzzy search instead. Type a few letters, press Enter, and you're there.

- Add to Chrome: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/kgjjlongdlmjcegnjicbpnkpnmoblkae
- Source: https://github.com/lx223/jumparound-browser-extension
